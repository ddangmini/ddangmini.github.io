SlaticHour Uploader

Developed with YouTube

SlaticHour Uploader is a private, single-user automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Static Hour (@StaticHour.shorts). It runs on the owner's own computer (n8n) and uploads short videos that the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel, together with their title, description and thumbnail.

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's data.

  • YouTube API usage: videos.insert, thumbnails.set, channels.list (own channel only)
