Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

SlaticHour Uploader ("the App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel SlaticHour (@SlaticHour.shorts) to upload the owner's own videos to that channel.

Information the App accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the App receives an OAuth token that allows it to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic channel information (channel ID, title and handle) of the owner's own channel through the YouTube API Services. The App does not access data of any other user.

How the information is used

The token and channel information are used only to confirm the target channel and to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails that the owner created. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored encrypted on the owner's own computer (self-hosted n8n). No data is stored on any other server operated by the App.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the App and the stored token becomes unusable.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
